Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

Muffin is an independently developed personal software application created and maintained by Rahul Gouri ("the Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains how Muffin accesses, uses, stores, and protects user information when you connect your Google Account to the Muffin Progressive Web App.

1. Information We Access and Process

Muffin requests access to your Google Account solely to enable core personal finance features:

  • Google Account Information: Your email address and basic profile identifier (used to create and identify your private session).
  • Google Sheets Data: Read and write access to the specific Google Sheet workbook you connect to Muffin (to log Income, Expense, and Investment transactions).
Google API Limited Use Disclosure: Muffin's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. Data Storage & Privacy

Your financial transaction records remain stored inside your personal Google Account spreadsheet in Google Drive. Muffin does not operate a central external database storing your bank statements, income, or transaction history. Recipe opening balances and linked spreadsheet IDs are saved to your user record in Netlify Blobs for multi-device sync.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Muffin does not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing partners. Data accessed from Google APIs is never used to train generalized AI or machine learning models.
