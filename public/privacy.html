Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

Muffin is an independently developed personal software application created and maintained by Rahul Gouri ("the Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains how Muffin accesses, uses, stores, and protects user information when you connect your Google Account to the Muffin Progressive Web App.

1. Information We Access and Process

Muffin requests access to your Google Account solely to enable core personal finance features:

  • Google Account Information: Your email address and basic profile identifier (used to create and identify your private session).
  • Google Sheets Data: Read and write access to the specific Google Sheet workbook you connect to Muffin (to log Income, Expense, and Investment transactions).
Google API Limited Use Disclosure: Muffin's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. Data Storage & Privacy

All your financial transaction records, opening balances, recurring rules, and investment baselines remain stored entirely inside your personal Google Account spreadsheet across dedicated Income, Expense, Investment, Recipe, and Rules tabs in Google Drive. Muffin does not operate a central external database storing your bank statements, income, or financial history. Only non-financial operational metadata (linked spreadsheet ID and title) is saved to your user record in Netlify Blobs.

3. Cookies, Sessions & Local Storage

Muffin uses browser storage technologies strictly for authentication and app operation:

  • Session Cookie (httpOnly): An encrypted session cookie (muffin_session) stores your authorization state securely so you stay signed in.
  • Local Storage: Your browser stores local preferences on your device, including selected theme (muffinTheme), display font (muffinFont), amount masking toggle (valuesMasked), local Recipe cache (muffinRecipe), and temporary Planner entries (plannerTransactions).

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Muffin does not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing partners. Data accessed from Google APIs is never used to train generalized AI or machine learning models.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your personal finance data:

  • Google Drive Data: You can edit or delete your financial records at any time directly inside your Google Sheet in Google Drive.
  • App Access Revocation: You can revoke Muffin's access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Security Settings.
  • Unlinking Sheet: You can unlink your spreadsheet in-app by clicking your spreadsheet title in the header.
  • Account Deletion: Logging out terminates your session. If you wish to permanently delete your linked configuration record from Netlify Blobs, contact the developer at rahulgouri072@gmail.com.

6. Data Retention

All financial transaction and recipe configuration data resides entirely in your personal Google Sheet and is retained as long as you maintain the sheet in Google Drive. Operational sheet connection metadata (linked sheet ID and title) is retained in Netlify Blobs until you request deletion or revoke account access.

7. Children's Privacy

Muffin is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

The Developer may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect updates in app functionality or regulatory guidelines. Revised policies will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
