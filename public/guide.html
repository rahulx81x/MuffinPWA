Classic Muffin Guide

Your cozy personal finance dashboard powered by Google Sheets and fast offline PWA capabilities.

⚡ Getting Started in 3 Steps

1. Sign In & Link Your Google Sheet ▼
Sign in using your Google Account, then paste an existing Google Sheet link/ID or tap "Create a sheet for me" to generate a pre-formatted 3-tab workbook automatically.
2. Log Transactions via In-App Forms or Sheets ▼
Tap the + button in the bottom floating navigation bar to log an Income, Expense, or Investment entry. You can also edit your Google Sheet directly — Muffin re-fetches automatically.
3. Explore FinTech Dashboard & Interactive Charts ▼
Tap any KPI tile on the Home tab to view interactive breakdown pie charts, monthly line trends with crosshairs, and Month-over-Month (MoM) delta callout cards.

📊 Google Sheet Structure

Muffin requires a single Google Sheet workbook with 3 fixed tabs:

1. Income Tab

Columns: Date, Category, Amount, Comment

Sample: 2026-08-01, Salary, 75000, Monthly pay

2. Expense Tab

Columns: Date, Category, Amount, Comment

Sample: 2026-08-02, Groceries, 3500, Supermarket

3. Investment Tab

Columns: Date, Category, Amount, Investment Type, Comment

Sample: 2026-08-05, SIP, 10000, Mutual Fund, Nifty Index

🧮 Built-In Expression Calculator

Inside the Amount field of the Add/Edit modal, you can type expressions directly:

➕ Standard Math

Type 1200 + 350 * 2 → evaluates to 1900

% Percentage & GST

Type 1000 * 18% → evaluates to 180

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my financial data shared with third parties? ▼
No. Your financial data stays inside your own personal Google Account spreadsheet. Muffin does not sell data, target ads, or train AI models.
How is Provident Fund (PF) handled? ▼
Investment rows tagged as Provident Fund, PF, EPF, or PPF are tracked in a dedicated Provident Fund total and excluded from short-term liquid metrics.
